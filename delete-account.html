Sahil Ai - Delete your account and data

Last updated: 2026-11-07

You can ask for your Sahil Ai chat account and your data to be deleted at any time. Deletion is free and you do not need to give a reason.

How to request deletion

1. Send a message to the owner: either inside the app's chat, or through the WhatsApp link inside the app.

2. In your message write: 'Delete my account', together with the email address you used to register in the app.

3. The owner deletes your account and data within 7 days and confirms to you when it is done.

What is deleted

Your account (name, email address, phone number, password and profile photo) and all of your chat messages, voice messages, photos and links are permanently deleted from Google Firebase. If you were a member of the owner's private group, your group messages are also deleted.

What is kept

Nothing is kept after deletion. There is no backup copy of your account or messages once they are deleted.

Contact: via the WhatsApp link or the chat inside the app.
