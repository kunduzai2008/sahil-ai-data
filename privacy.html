Sahil Ai - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-07

Sahil Ai contains no analytics and no advertising.

The app shows the owner's public links (YouTube, TikTok, TikTok Plugin file, WhatsApp channel, WhatsApp, Facebook and Telegram). Tapping a link opens it in the relevant app or browser on your device; those services have their own privacy policies.

The chat

The app has an optional built-in chat. If you choose to use it, you create an account with your name, email address, phone number and a password, and you may add a profile photo. That information — together with the text, voice messages, photos and links you send — is stored in Google Firebase so the chat can work and the owner can reply to you. Your chat is private: only you and the owner can see it. This information is never sold and never shared with anyone else. If you never open the chat, none of this information is collected.

If the owner invites you to his private group inside the chat and you accept, your name, profile photo and group messages are visible to the other members of that group.

The chat's voice and video call option is currently switched off. If it is enabled in a future version, calls will be carried by Agora; the audio and video of a call pass through Agora's servers while the call lasts and are not recorded or stored by the app.

The app also downloads one small public file to show the NEW badges on the link banners; that file contains no personal data.

Contact: via the WhatsApp link or the chat inside the app.
